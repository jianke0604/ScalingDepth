A short history

From identity shortcuts to attention over depth

2015Highway Networks & ResNet. The identity shortcut makes 100+ layers trainable.
2017Post-LN Transformer. DenseNet shows the value of dense cross-layer reuse.
2020Pre-LN, ReZero, Admin, Fixup. Training stability at depth.
2022DeepNorm scales Post-LN Transformers to 1,000 layers.
2023–25Depth-µP, CompleteP, Hyper-Connections, DenseFormer, mHC. Depth parametrization and richer residual streams.
2026AttnRes, MoDA, KEEL, SiameseNorm, KiteNorm. Attention over depth and Post-LN revisited.
